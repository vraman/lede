# Contributing

Thanks for your interest. Lede is a personal project I build in my spare time, so I can't promise a timeline or that every PR will land — but input is genuinely welcome.

**Bugs and ideas** — open an issue. I'll get to it when I can.

**Pull requests** — smaller is easier to review. A one-line note on why the change is needed helps a lot. If it touches a lot of files or adds a major feature, opening an issue first lets us agree on direction before you invest the time.

MIT license applies to anything you contribute. No CLA, no bots.
